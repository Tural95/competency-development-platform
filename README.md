# Competency Development Platform

> An independently developed competency assessment and development ecosystem supporting more than 1,000 learners, educators, and professionals in Azerbaijan.

## Overview

The Competency Development Platform is designed to connect competency assessment with structured, measurable development. It helps transform fragmented learning activities into a practical development ecosystem where capability gaps can be identified, development actions can be planned, and progress can be monitored over time.

The project has been independently designed, tested, and refined over six months. Its methodologies, learning solutions, and related platforms are currently used by:

- **600+ students and educators**
- **400+ professionals**
- **1,000+ users in total**

## The Gap It Fills

Many educational and professional development environments rely on disconnected assessments, training activities, and manual development plans. This makes it difficult to:

- connect assessment results with relevant learning actions;
- create consistent competency-based development plans;
- measure progress beyond training attendance;
- provide accessible development tools for institutions and individuals;
- turn identified skill gaps into clear, actionable interventions.

This project addresses that gap by bringing assessment, development planning, learning interventions, and progress tracking into one structured framework.

## Intended Users

- Universities and educators

- Learning and development centers
- HR and talent development teams
- Trainers and facilitators

## Core Capabilities

- Competency framework and profile management
- Structured competency assessments
- Gap identification and prioritization
- Individual Development Plans (IDPs)
- Recommended learning and development activities
- Progress and evidence tracking
- Role-aware workflows
- Institutional reporting and insights
- Scalable learning and development program design

## Current Impact

The project is not only a concept. The platforms, learning solutions, assessment approaches, and development programs created through this work are already supporting more than **1,000 people** across academic and professional communities.

Its impact is based on a multiplier model: one platform strengthens educators, students, and professionals, who then apply and share the acquired knowledge in their own classrooms, teams, organizations, and communities.

## Development Status

The platform is under active development and refinement. The current focus is on:

1. strengthening the competency assessment methodology;
2. improving personalized development planning;
3. increasing usability and accessibility;
4. introducing secure and role-aware workflows;
5. improving testing, documentation, and scalability;
6. preparing a reliable public-facing release.

## Architecture Direction

The production-oriented architecture uses:

- **GitHub** for version control and development workflow;
- **Vercel** for web deployment;
- **Supabase** for database, authentication, and secure backend services;
- AI-assisted workflows for structured recommendations, content support, and development planning.

Sensitive production code, credentials, personal data, and institution-specific business logic are not included in this public showcase repository.

## Responsible AI and Privacy

The project is being designed around the following principles:

- human oversight for consequential decisions;
- transparent recommendations;
- minimum necessary data collection;
- role-based access control;
- privacy-aware handling of learner and employee information;
- no publication of real personal or institutional data;
- secure management of API keys and environment variables.

## Repository Purpose

This public repository presents the project’s purpose, impact, roadmap, and architecture direction. It does not expose:

- production credentials or API keys;
- real student, educator, employee, or institutional data;
- private business logic;
- proprietary integrations;
- confidential deployment configuration.
